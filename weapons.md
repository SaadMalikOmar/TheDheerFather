# Weapons (Runner)

> **CRITICAL OPEN QUESTION:** What do these damage numbers apply to? Killers are listed with
> **Kealth** (knockout health), not death-health. Recommended model: runner weapons **stun /
> knock out** killers to buy time — they do not kill. Damage numbers below would then deplete a
> killer's Kealth toward a temporary knockout. Confirm before tuning. See
> [design-pillars.md](design-pillars.md).

## The Axon (Axe)

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Damage | 67 |
| Speed | Slow |
| Range | Medium |
| Durability | High |
| Effect | **Bleed out** (damage over time) |

- Heavy, reliable melee. High durability = the "main" weapon.
- *Question:* does Bleed out work on non-human killers? (Skinny Bear / Tung Tung.)

## Triple T Bat

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Damage | 70 |
| Speed | Slow |
| Range | Far |
| Durability | **Low (2 hits)** |
| Effect | **Stun** |

- Highest damage + longest melee range, but breaks after 2 hits → burst/panic weapon.

## Lip Stick Tazer

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Damage | 10 |
| Speed | 1 use |
| Range | Far |
| Durability | **1 use** |
| Effect | **Stun — human killers only** |

- Ranged panic button, but **only affects Lucki** (the only human killer).
  Skinny Bear and Tung Tung are tase-immune ("not human").
- *Balance flag:* a one-use ranged tool that's useless vs 2 of 3 killers may feel bad to carry.
  Consider: give it a *minor* effect vs non-human killers (brief flinch), or make it a guaranteed
  Lucki counter that's worth a loadout slot specifically against him.

## Gaps / ideas

- Only 3 weapons so far, all anti-killer. Consider **utility items** (not just weapons) for the
  item shop: flashlights, noise-makers, smoke, lockpicks, healing items (overlaps Mahnam).
- Define how runners **acquire** weapons (spawn with one? find in houses? buy in shop and bring in?).
